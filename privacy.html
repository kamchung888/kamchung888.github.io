Privacy Policy

Applies to HK Talks Uploader and HK Talks Analytics. Effective 27 September 2026.

Home Terms of Service

1. Who we are

HK Talks Tools are private tools used by the owner of the YouTube channel 港人講事 (HK Talks) to manage his own channel. They are not offered to the public. The only person who signs in to them is the channel owner.

2. YouTube API Services

These tools use YouTube API Services. By using them you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. What we access

When the owner signs in with Google, the tools receive permission to act on his YouTube account:

  • HK Talks Uploader (permissions: youtube.upload, youtube): uploads the owner's own video files; sets their title, description, tags, category, thumbnail, audience setting and the "altered or synthetic content" disclosure; reads back the status of the videos it uploaded; reads the channel name to confirm it is signed in to the correct channel.
  • HK Talks Analytics (permission: youtube.readonly): reads statistics and analytics reports for the owner's own channel and videos. It cannot change anything.

The tools do not access comments, subscribers' personal information, messages, or any other person's account.

4. How we use it

  • To publish the channel's own episodes with the settings the owner chose.
  • To show the owner how his own videos are performing, so he can plan future episodes.

We do not use the information for advertising, and we do not sell, rent or share it with anyone. Information is sent to Google/YouTube through YouTube API Services, as needed to perform the actions above.

AI assistant. The owner runs these tools with the help of an AI assistant (Claude, provided by Anthropic). The assistant may process the channel's own upload records and statistics on the owner's behalf, for example to report on how an episode is performing. It does not receive the Google sign-in tokens, and the data is not used for any other purpose.

5. What we store, and where

  • Sign-in tokens. The Google authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in files readable only by the owner. They are kept for as long as the owner keeps the tools authorized.
  • Upload records. For each upload we keep the video ID, link, title, time and privacy setting in the channel's local production folder, so the same episode is not uploaded twice.
  • Channel statistics. Analytics reports for the owner's channel are kept on the owner's computer for his own planning. We re-check the authorization at least every 30 days and refresh or delete stored data in line with the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

Nothing is stored on this website or on any other server run by us.

6. Cookies and third parties

The tools do not place cookies or other tracking on any device, and no third party serves advertising through them. This website itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Revoking access and deleting data

  • You can revoke the tools' access to your Google account at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • After access is revoked, we delete the stored tokens and any stored data obtained through YouTube API Services within 30 days.
  • To ask us to delete stored data at any time, email the address below. We will delete it within 7 days.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

Questions or complaints about privacy: kamchung888@gmail.com
