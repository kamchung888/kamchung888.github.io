HK Talks Tools · 港人講事工具

Tools for the 港人講事 YouTube channel

Private, single-operator tools that help the owner of the 港人講事 (HK Talks) channel publish and review his own videos.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service The channel on YouTube

港人講事 is a Cantonese-language YouTube channel that publishes one current-affairs episode a day from a Hong Kong perspective. Each episode is produced on the channel owner's own computer. The tools below handle the last step, putting the finished episode on YouTube, so that it happens the same way every day.

These tools use YouTube API Services. They are not a public service. Only the channel owner signs in, and they only work with the owner's own channel.

HK Talks Uploader

Uploads a finished episode from the owner's computer to the 港人講事 channel.

  • Takes the episode's title, description, tags and thumbnail from the channel's own production files.
  • Marks the video as containing altered or synthetic content, because episodes include AI-generated illustrations.
  • Marks the video as not made for kids and sets the category to News & Politics.
  • Uploads as private by default. A video is made public or scheduled only when the owner chooses to.

Google sign-in: the owner authorizes access to his YouTube account once. The tool uses that access only to upload videos, set their thumbnails and settings, and confirm it is signed in to the right channel.

HK Talks Analytics

Reads the channel's own viewing statistics (read-only), so the owner can see which episodes work and plan new ones. It cannot change anything on YouTube.

Your data

The tools collect no information from viewers or from anyone other than the channel owner. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is accessed and stored, and how to revoke access. Use of the tools is subject to the Terms of Service, which include the YouTube Terms of Service.
